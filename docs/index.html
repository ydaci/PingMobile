Velociping Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 18, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Velociping handles information when you use the Velociping mobile game.

1. Developer

Velociping is developed and published by Yanis Daci.

For privacy-related questions, you can contact the developer through the contact information provided on the Google Play Store listing.

2. Information collected

Velociping does not require you to create an account and does not directly ask you to provide personal information such as your name, postal address or telephone number.

However, Velociping uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) to display advertisements. The Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect certain information from your device for advertising, analytics and fraud-prevention purposes.

This may include:

  • IP address, which may be used to estimate the general location of the device;
  • Android advertising ID and other device or app identifiers;
  • Information about interactions with the application, such as app launches, taps and video views;
  • Diagnostic and performance information, such as application launch time, crashes, hang information and energy usage;
  • Information relating to advertisements displayed or interacted with.

3. How information is used

Information processed through Google Mobile Ads may be used for purposes including:

  • Displaying and personalizing advertisements;
  • Measuring advertising performance;
  • Analytics and improving advertising services;
  • Preventing fraud and abuse;
  • Improving the performance and reliability of the advertising SDK.

4. Third-party services

Velociping uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob), a service provided by Google, to display advertisements.

Google may collect and process information as described in its own privacy documentation. Data collected by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit.

For more information about Google's privacy practices, please visit:

Google Privacy Policy

5. Advertising ID

Android provides users with controls over their advertising ID. Depending on the device and Android version, users may reset or delete their advertising ID through the Android settings.

6. Data retention

Velociping itself does not maintain a personal user account or a personal database containing information provided directly by users.

Information processed by third-party services such as Google Mobile Ads is subject to those services' respective retention policies.

7. Children's privacy

Velociping is not specifically directed toward children. The application does not knowingly request personal information from children.

8. Security

Velociping does not directly transmit personal information to its own servers. Information processed by Google Mobile Ads is transmitted using encryption in transit.

9. Changes to this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes to the application, its services or applicable legal requirements. Any updated version will be published on this page.

10. Contact

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy or Velociping's privacy practices, please contact the developer through the contact information available on the application's Google Play Store listing.
